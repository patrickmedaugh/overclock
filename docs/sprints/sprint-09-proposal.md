# Sprint 09 proposal — Traces, tags, no stuck games

**Reply "approved" to start, or comment on any story to change it.**

## Goal
The Corp can now **hunt the Runner down**. Some cards start a **trace**, a quick bidding contest. If the Corp wins it, the Runner gets a **tag**. While he is tagged, the Corp can trash his resources, and **Private Security Force** finally works. Kate's printed **1 link** now helps her in traces. Four new real Core Set cards bring traces into both decks. In online games, a player whose device drops out no longer leaves the other player stuck: after a short wait, the game answers for them. Everything works in pass-the-phone games and online games.

## The new rules in plain words
- **Trace** = a bidding contest started by a card. The card shows a number, the trace's starting strength (Hunter: **Trace 3**). The Corp spends credits to add to it, 1 point per credit. Then the Runner spends credits to add to his **link** (Kate's printed number, 1, plus any link cards he has). If the Corp's total is **higher**, the trace succeeds and the card's effect happens (Hunter: the Runner gets 1 tag). If the Runner's total is **equal or higher**, nothing happens. Credits spent are gone either way.
  - *Example:* Hunter fires, Trace 3. The Corp adds 2 credits: strength 5. Kate has link 1, so she must spend 4 credits to reach 5 and stop it.
- **In the real game, the Corp bids first, out loud, and then the Runner answers.** Our earlier notes said "secret bids, revealed together", which is not how the real game works. Question 1 lets you pick. We recommend the real rule.
- **Tag** = the Corp has found where the Runner lives. Tags stay until he removes them. While the Runner has at least 1 tag:
  - the Corp may spend **1 click + 2 credits** to trash one of his resources (for example Armitage Codebusting or Bank Job);
  - Private Security Force gains **"Click: Do 1 meat damage."**
- **Removing a tag:** on his turn the Runner may spend **1 click + 2 credits** to remove 1 tag.
- **Meat damage** = like net damage: the Runner throws away a random card from his hand for each point. If he has to throw away more cards than he holds, he is **flatlined** and the Corp wins.

## Stories

### S-09-1: Traces: the bidding contest, with Hunter
*Why it matters:* this is the biggest rule left in the Core Set, and many real cards need it.
*Acceptance criteria:* (written for question 1 = A, the real rule; if you pick B we'll rewrite them for secret bids)
- Given a face-up **Hunter** and the Runner can't break it, when he lets its subroutine fire, then a trace screen opens for the Corp: "Trace 3: add credits?" with the bid control from question 2, showing the total as it changes (3 + bid) and a **Bid** button.
- Given the Corp bid 2 (strength 5), when it is the Runner's turn to answer, then he sees "Trace strength 5 · your link 1 · spend 4 to stop it", picks his own bid and taps **Bid**.
- Given both bids are in, when the trace resolves, then a message shows both totals, for example "Hunter: trace 5 vs link 5: Runner escaped" or "trace 5 vs link 3: Runner tagged". Both players' credits go down by what they bid.
- Given the Runner's link plus bid **equals** the trace strength, when it resolves, then the trace fails and he gets no tag (ties go to the Runner, as in the real rules). (automated check)
- Given pass-the-phone and a trace during the Runner's run, when the Corp must bid, then the cover screen hands the phone to the Corp, and after its bid hands it back to the Runner. Neither player sees the other's hand.
- Given a player has 0 credits, when it is their turn to bid, then the game bids 0 for them straight away, with no hand-off. (automated check)
- Given an online game, when a trace happens, then the Corp bids on its device while the Runner sees "Corp is choosing its trace bid…", then the Runner bids on his. Both devices show the same result as in pass-the-phone. (automated check)
- Given a small phone (640×360), when the bid screen is up, then the numbers are readable and the buttons are easy to tap.
*Size:* L

### S-09-2: Tags: trash a resource, remove a tag
*Why it matters:* this is what a trace actually does to the Runner, and it gives the Corp a new way to attack.
*Acceptance criteria:*
- Given the Runner has 1 tag, when either player looks at the Runner's counters, then a **"Tagged 1"** marker shows next to his credits on both screens.
- Given the Runner is tagged and has Armitage Codebusting installed, when the Corp taps Armitage on its own turn, then a **Trash · 1 click + 2 credits** button appears. Tapping it puts Armitage in the Runner's Heap (discard pile), with a message.
- Given the Runner is not tagged, when the Corp taps a Runner resource, then no trash button is offered. (automated check)
- Given the Runner is tagged, when he taps the tag marker on his turn, then a **Remove 1 tag · 1 click + 2 credits** button appears and lowers the count by 1. With too few credits or no clicks left, it is dimmed and says why.
- Given the Runner has tags, when turns pass, then the tags stay until he removes them. (automated check)
- Given an online game, when the Corp trashes a resource or the Runner removes a tag, then both devices show the same result as in pass-the-phone. (automated check)
*Size:* M

### S-09-3: Private Security Force works (meat damage)
*Why it matters:* this is the last card in the decks that didn't fully work.
*Acceptance criteria:*
- Given the Corp has scored Private Security Force and the Runner is tagged, when the Corp taps it in its score area on its turn, then a **Do 1 meat damage · 1 click** button appears. Tapping it throws away 1 random card from the Runner's Grip (hand) to his Heap, with a message naming the card.
- Given the Corp has more clicks, when it taps the button again, then it works again, once per click.
- Given the Runner is not tagged, when the Corp taps Private Security Force, then the button is dimmed and says "Runner isn't tagged".
- Given the Runner has no cards in his Grip, when he takes meat damage, then he is flatlined and the Corp wins. (automated check)
- Given an online game, when the Corp uses it, then both devices show the same lost card face up in the Heap. (automated check)
*Size:* S

### S-09-4: New trace cards: Ichi 1.0, SEA Source, Access to Globalsec, and the deck changes
*Why it matters:* traces show up in real games from both sides, and Kate gets a way to fight back.
*Acceptance criteria:*
- Given a face-up **Ichi 1.0** and the Runner has two programs, when he lets all three subroutines fire, then the Corp picks a program to trash twice (Sacrificial Construct can save one, as with Rototurret), and then a Trace 1 runs. If it succeeds, the Runner gets 1 tag and 1 brain damage.
- Given the Runner made a successful run on his last turn, when the Corp plays **SEA Source** (cost 2), then a Trace 3 starts. If it succeeds, the Runner gets 1 tag.
- Given the Runner made no successful run on his last turn, when the Corp looks at SEA Source in its hand, then it is dimmed and says "Runner made no successful run last turn".
- Given Kate has one **Access to Globalsec** installed, when you look at her link, then it shows **2** (3 with two copies), and the next trace uses that number.
- Given the new deck lists, when they are checked, then each has 30 cards and is legal: Corp influence 8 of 15 with 15 agenda points, Runner influence 10 of 15. (automated check)
- Given every card in both decks, when its text is checked against the card data, then each one works as the table above says. (automated check)
*Size:* M

### ~~S-09-5: Online: the game answers for a player who drops out~~ — DROPPED (owner, 2026-11-16: not needed now; he plays with friends and would rather save a game when someone leaves — backlog)
*Why it matters:* today, if one device drops out at the wrong moment, the other player waits with "Waiting for the other player to come back…" and nothing happens.
*How it behaves:* this only happens when a player's device is **away**, meaning the app is closed, the phone is locked long enough to disconnect, or the signal is lost. A player who is still connected and just thinking is never hurried. The other player sees a countdown ("The Corp is away. The game answers for them in 30 s"). If the away player comes back in time, they answer as usual. If not, the game gives a careful answer (question 4) and tells both players what it chose.
*Acceptance criteria:*
- Given an online game where the Corp's device is away, when the Runner approaches a face-down ICE, then he sees the countdown. When it ends, the ICE stays face down (**Don't rez**) and the run goes on.
- Given the same, when Rototurret's or Ichi 1.0's "Trash 1 program" fires and the Runner has 2 or more programs, then when the countdown ends the game trashes the program that cost the most to install (if two are tied, the one installed first). The Runner sees which one went.
- Given the Runner's device is away, when a program would be trashed and Sacrificial Construct could save it, then when the countdown ends the game saves the program (trashes Sacrificial Construct instead).
- Given either player's device is away at a trace bid, when the countdown ends, then the game bids 0 credits for them.
- Given the away player comes back, when they open the game again, then a note says what the game chose for them, for example "While you were away: Don't rez on Hunter".
- Given the away player comes back before the countdown ends, when they answer, then their answer counts, as today. (automated check)
- Given an away Corp, when the countdown runs, then it lasts exactly as long whether or not the Corp could have paid to rez, so the wait gives away nothing about its credits. (automated check)
- Given a player whose device is connected, when they take a long time to answer, then the game never answers for them. (automated check)
*Size:* M

## Questions for you
If you approve without answering, the recommended options apply. You can answer in one line, e.g. "1a 2a 3a 4a 5a approved".

1. **How do trace bids work?** — **Recommended:** A. Options: A) **the real rule.** The Corp bids first, and the Runner sees the Corp's bid before choosing his. This is simpler on one phone and in online games. B) **secret bids.** Both bid without seeing the other's, then the bids are revealed together. This is a house rule, a bit more of a guessing game, and it needs an extra hand-off in pass-the-phone.
2. **How do you enter a bid on the phone?** — **Recommended:** A. Options: A) a big number with **−** and **+** buttons on either side, a **Max** button, and **Bid** (each tap adds or takes away 1 credit). B) a row of number buttons, 0 up to your credits (fastest for small bids, but cramped with 15+ credits). C) a slider you drag (quick, but harder to hit an exact number with a thumb).
3. **How long does the game wait for an away player before answering for them?** — **Recommended:** A. Options: A) **30 seconds** (enough to switch apps and come back). B) **60 seconds** (kinder on bad signal, longer to wait). C) **15 seconds** (fastest, but a short app switch could cost you a choice).
4. **What does the game answer for them?** — **Recommended:** A. Options: A) **careful answers:** Don't rez, bid 0, save the program with Sacrificial Construct, and the Corp's Rototurret / Ichi pick takes the Runner's most expensive program. B) the same, but the Corp's pick is a random program. C) **don't answer at all.** Keep waiting as today (this story is dropped).
5. **Which new cards?** — **Recommended:** A. Options: A) **as in the table:** Hunter ×2, Ichi 1.0, SEA Source for the Corp, and Access to Globalsec ×2 for Kate. B) **smaller:** only Hunter ×2 and Access to Globalsec ×2. Ichi 1.0 and SEA Source wait for next sprint. C) **A plus two harsher cards:** Scorched Earth for the Corp ("Do 4 meat damage" to a tagged Runner, often a game-ender) and Decoy for Kate (avoid 1 tag). Scorched Earth replaces one Hedge Fund and Decoy replaces one Diesel. Both decks stay legal, with Corp influence 12 and Runner 12.

## Risks
- **Card text is typed from memory**, as in earlier sprints. The numbers are checked against the official list. The wording is checked against the 2012 printing during the build. If anything looks wrong to you, tell us and it becomes a quick fix.
- **Traces add hand-offs in pass-the-phone.** A trace during a run means passing the phone to the Corp and back. To keep it quick, nobody is asked to bid when they have no credits to spend.
- **Tags plus Private Security Force can end a game fast.** Three meat damage in one Corp turn can flatline a Runner with a small hand. That's the real game. We'll show the tag marker clearly so being tagged is never a surprise. Tell us at the demo if it feels too harsh.
- **An automatic answer might not be what the away player would have chosen.** That's why the answers are careful and both players are told what was chosen.
- **Online games running during the update will end.** The rules and decks change, so the online service can't continue a game started on the old rules. It ends cleanly with "That game has ended", and you start a new one. *(Team note: bump `RULES_VERSION` in `packages/engine/src/state.ts` from 2 to 3 with this sprint's rules and card changes.)*
- **The red "Workers Builds" check** on GitHub comes from your Cloudflare connection, not our tests. We're still ignoring it, as you asked.
- **If the sprint runs long,** SEA Source and Ichi 1.0 move to Sprint 10 first. Their deck slots go back to Anonymous Tip and the second Rototurret, so the decks stay legal. We'll say so in the demo notes.
