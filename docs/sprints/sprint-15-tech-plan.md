## Movement (client only; the engine stays pure)
- **One scheduler:** the existing skippable `fx` `Tweens` queue. Input feedback (press lift, lean, drag follow, hover) goes on `ui` (never skipped). **Tap finishes:** `main.ts` adds a capture-phase `pointerdown` listener on the canvas's parent that calls `fx.finishAll()` before Pixi dispatches the tap (logs `motion_skipped`), so a tap always acts on the final board. Resize/rotate already finishes (`redrawAll`). `fx` never blocks input; every record's `scheduledMs` ≤ 600.
- **Pure planner `apps/client/src/motion.ts`** (no Pixi; pinned, ui-dev pushes the types + stub first):
  `planMotion(viewer: Side, events: readonly GameEvent[]): Flight[]`, `Flight = {kind: FlightKind; cardId: string | null /* null = drawn face down */; side: Side; from: Anchor; to: Anchor; delayMs; durationMs; land?: {counter: 'deck'|'discard'|'score'|'hand'; side: Side}}`, `Anchor = {at: 'hand'; side; index?} | {at: 'deck'|'discard'|'score'; side} | {at: 'installed'; uid} | {at: 'center'} | {at: 'drop'}`, `FlightKind = 'draw'|'install'|'play'|'showcase'|'discard'|'trash'|'score'|'steal'|'damage'|'mill'|'return'`, `motionSpan(f: Flight[]) = max(delay+duration)`. **`cardId` comes only from the viewer's projected event** (`viewEvents`), never from the view of the other seat, so a Corp install/discard/ABT mill seen by the Runner is a card back. The board resolves anchors to boxes (opponent's hand = top edge, `drop` = the release point). Runs keep their Sprint 3–12 animations (next slice).
- **Timings (`theme.timing`, Q1 A):** flight 240 ms, landing settle 110 ms (scale 0.92→1, `outBack`; total ≤ 360), showcase 180 → center, hold 150, 180 → discard (510), press lift 80 ms (scale 1.08, −10 % card height), return glide 160 ms, score tick 200 ms + glow 300 ms. **Ripple:** stagger `min(60, 260/(n−1))` ms so a batch ends ≤ 500 ms; at most 4 sprites in the air, extra cards land at once (counts still right). Lean: `leanAngle(vx px/ms, prev, dt)` pure in `handGesture.ts`, `±14°` max, eased back to 0 in ~80 ms when still. Decorative extras (settle, lean, hover) obey `framesAreSlow()` like the carousel (S-05-3).
- **Pile counts change on landing:** piles/score widgets show the pre-batch value and `pop`/tick on each `land` (pattern of S-10-5's Archives); `finishAll` lands everything, so a skipped or rotated batch ends exact.
- **Other seat's cards:** online, the other phone's moves arrive as `same` presentations: install/play flights start at the top hand area. Pass-the-phone: `Presentation.handoff` gains `missedEvents: GameEvent[]` (the next seat's projection of the actor's whole turn, collected by `HotSeat`; not saved), played as one ripple (≤ 500 ms) after the cover lifts.
- **Cost:** flights reuse the baked card textures (no new `Graphics` per flight); face-down = the shared back texture.
- **Test switch (test build only):** `?motion=instant|hold` and hook `__overclock.motion(mode)` (`normal` default, so existing specs keep exercising real motion via `waitIdle`); `hold` freezes `fx` (not `ui`) and `__overclock.advanceMotion(ms)` steps it, so specs inspect mid-flight and tap deterministically. `layout()` gains `motion: {active: number; flights: {kind, cardId: string|null, box}[]}`. New `AnimKind`s: `hand_press`, `hand_return`, `land_settle`, `showcase`, `score_tick`, `opponent_flight`, `motion_skipped`.

## S-15-6 addendum (hold to play)
Client only: no engine/card/protocol/save change, no new actions or events (`RULES_VERSION` 5, `PROTOCOL_VERSION` 2). Every lit place and every reason still comes from `view.handOptions` (`reject` worded by `hintFor`/`rejectText`); the client never decides legality.
- **Gesture model (Hearthstone's):** *sideways = browse, still hold or upward = pick up*, in **both** hand views. Pure `classifyPress` in `handGesture.ts`, judged by the press's own event times (`madeAt`), never by when a timer ran (QA-S3-7). **Hand view (big card):** inside 12 px and up − down < `HOLD_MS` = 250 → **tap** ("Hold to play" tip, `hold_tip`; no pick, no lit places, view stays); still for 250 ms → **lift**; leaving the 12 px circle within 40° of straight up (−dy ≥ 1.2·|dx|) → **lift at once**; any other direction → **swipe** (browse, as today). A timer that ran late followed by a quick release is a tap (the lift is undone at once). **Small hand strip:** tap = open the view (as today); still hold = read big + slide to browse (S-04-3, `longPress` 350 ms unchanged: the 40×56 px cards at 891×296 are unreadable, so a still hold must stay "read"); **upward out of the strip** = lift — before the hold (same 40° rule at 12 px) or during the hold-read (finger above `handArea.y − 0.15·card.h`: lifts the card being read). So grabbing from the strip **is in scope** (AC 2 amended, DECISIONS): the strip's slide is sideways along a 56 px row, which the upward rule never confuses. No pick-up in the discard step (S-10-5 as today).
- **Lift:** the card follows the finger (`ghostCenter`, lean), the big view fades (100 ms), the strip **drops below the screen** (`Hand.minimize`, 120 ms, `hand_minimize`/`hand_restore` on `ui`; the lifted sprite is not moved). Exception: a card whose options light Grip cards (Modded) keeps the strip up. One-shot cards with one option light a **play zone** = the table above the hand, left of the rail (`layout.playZone`, pure, unit-tested; nested targets still win by `targetAt`'s smallest box); two-mode cards keep their labelled buttons. **Infiltration:** while held, "Gain 2" and every exposable face-down card light at once (drop on the card = expose); the separate "Expose 1 card" step goes (it needed the sticky tap pick). Hovered place = strong highlight (`setHover`, snapshot `targets[].hover`). Unplayable (`refusePick` ≠ null, incl. a choice pending): lifts **dimmed**, nothing lit, hint from the existing reason; release returns it. Drop on lit = its option's action (unchanged `dropLift`); elsewhere or dimmed = return glide 140 ms + hand back (+ reason when dimmed). Tap-to-play (`pickCard`, the hand glow) is **removed**.
- **Arrow (try, then decide):** one arrow from the held card's top edge to the hovered place's price pill (where the card lands), via pure `arrowFor(from, to, litBoxes, viewport)` → segment or null: null if shorter than 0.5·card height, off screen, crossing another lit place, or `framesAreSlow()`. U9 measures at 891×296 (Corp busy board + Runner rig: share of hover points with an arrow; screenshots for the demo). If it crowds, `ARROW = false` and the highlight alone stays (first to cut).
- **Mouse:** the same Pixi pointer handlers (press-hold or drag up with the button down); hover rise stays; no click-to-play.
- **Online / hidden info:** a held card is local UI state; nothing is sent until the drop's one action (as S-07-6). Automated: `s15-online` holds a card over a lit place for 1 s and asserts the other phone received no game frame and its view is unchanged, then sees only the played card (face down for a Corp install).
- **Test hooks:** snapshot `drag` gains `dimmed`, `reason`, `arrow`; `handMinimized`; `selected` = the held index while a playable card is held (so old asserts read the same). **The shortcut lives in the e2e helpers, not the product:** `pickHand`/`pickCard` do a real hold on the strip + a 3-step upward drag (lifts at once, no 250 ms wait) and keep the finger down; `tap()`/`sendTap()` while a card is held move that finger there and let go (= the drop). So the ~150 `pickCard … tap(targetOf(…))` uses migrate with no spec edit and still drive the real gesture; only specs that kept a pick across other taps (switching picks, Infiltration's Expose step, Modded) are rewritten. Rejected: a test-build hook that keeps tap-to-play (the old path would stay alive and the real gesture would go untested).

**Owner round 3 (2026-11-22):** *"The hold to drag a card around still takes far too long … Anything longer than a quick tap should grab the card. And the card should still be bigger. … directly dragging a card from a minimized hand did not work either."* Changes (client only; engine, cards, protocol untouched; DECISIONS 2026-11-22):
- **Grab speed:** `HOLD_MS` 250 → **120 ms** (a quick tap is ~50–100 ms); the move circle `SWIPE_TOLERANCE` 12 → **7 px**. Hand view: leaving the circle within ~51° of straight up (`UP_RATIO` 1.2 → **0.8**, so a 45° diagonal grabs) lifts at once; flatter = browse (unchanged). Small hand strip: **a still hold of 120 ms lifts the card too**, and **any move out of the circle except a mostly-downward one lifts it** (`stripPickUp(dx, dy)`; the strip has no sideways browse of its own). Held still and let go where it was pressed (a slow click, a hold to read): it goes back at once and counts as a tap (strip: the hand view opens on it). The strip's hold-to-read + slide (S-04-3, 350 ms) stays only where nothing can be picked up (the discard step). The lift stays one frame after the timer (≤ 16 ms, so a queued quick release is a tap); the late-timer catch-up (QA-S15-2) is unchanged and now also covers the strip (`onHoldLift`/`onLiftUndo` in `attachPress`, judged by event times).
- **Held size:** `heldScale` = min(1 × big card, **0.63** × screen height) (was 0.75 / 0.42): 1.5× at every size — 844×390: 164 → 246 px tall (176 wide); 640×360: 151 → 227 px (164 wide); 891×296: 124 → 186 px; desktop 1280×800: 336 → 504 px. Placed above the finger (beside it near the top), always wholly on screen; it may cover the hand. The held card shows a face drawn at the held size (made on the press, baked once per card and size), so its name stays sharp at 3×.
- **Drag from the small hand (root cause):** with the hand view open the small hand still shows at the bottom, but the view took every press and treated a press on a small card as a browse (`viewHit` → `card`, a move → swipe), so it could never be picked up; and with the view closed a slow (paced) drag passed the 350 ms read-hold first, whose slide switched the read card under the moving finger, so a neighbouring card was lifted. Now a press on a small-hand card under the view follows the strip's rule (`viewHit` marks `strip`), and the strip lifts at 120 ms on the pressed card. Tests: `s15-owner` "round 3" (real CDP touch / mouse, paced 4 px steps, view closed and open, hold then drag, quick tap) fail on the old code; `input.test.ts` hold-lift cases; `handGesture.test.ts` new numbers.
